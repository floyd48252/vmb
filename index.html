About Habesh

Comfort for the journey, care at every stop.

Habesh Luxury Coach connects travelers across the Nairobi–Addis Ababa corridor and regional destinations. We aim to make long-distance travel more comfortable, with thoughtful onboard amenities and clear route information.

Our coaches offer reclining leather seats, generous legroom, Wi-Fi, USB charging, climate control, and luggage storage. Whether you are traveling between cities or crossing the Kenya–Ethiopia border, our team can help you plan the details of your trip.
